How ExpenseGuard decides

Simple, explainable JavaScript logic for your first evaluation.

1
Group by category

Transactions are collected into category arrays using objects and forEach().

2
Calculate average

reduce() calculates the mean amount for the relevant spending group.

3
Measure unusualness

A z-score compares an expense with its normal range. Larger absolute values mean more unusual.

4
Assign risk

High and medium flags are shown with reasons so the result is easy to explain.
